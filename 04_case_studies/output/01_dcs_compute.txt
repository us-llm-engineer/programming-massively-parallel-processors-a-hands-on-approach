==================== DIRECT COULOMB SUMMATION: RESULTS ====================
GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 Ti | book source: PMPP Chapter 9 (in this edition Chapter 8 is MRI)

---- The problem ----
  potential(j) = sum_i q_i / sqrt((xj-xi)^2 + (yj-yi)^2 + (zj-zi)^2)
  lattice spacing 1.0 A, atoms uniform in the lattice box, charges uniform in [-1,+1].
  Padded-lattice cells are computed but not counted as useful work.
  Error metric: |GPU - double CPU| / sum(|q|/r)  (charges cancel, so plain relative error misleads).
