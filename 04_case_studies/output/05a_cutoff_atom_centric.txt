How to read:
  * Volume grows per row; atoms and lattice points grow with it (fixed density).
  * [book] direct summation is O(Volume^2) and cutoff summation O(Volume). The exponents above are what this run shows;
    small volumes deviate because spheres are clipped by the domain faces (less work per atom) and the GPU is
    under-filled, so judge the trend on the LAST steps.
  * 'scat/CPU' is the GPU scatter speedup over the sequential CPU atom-centric code; 'direct/scat' > 1 means DirectSum
    is slower than scatter at that size.
  * The atoms are placed at random, so few threads update the same lattice point at the same moment; the book's
    atomicAdd contention worry is therefore mild in this data. It is judged against the bin versions in 05b-05d.
