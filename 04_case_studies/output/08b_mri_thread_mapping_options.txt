What the numbers say [MEASURED]:
  * Option 1 uses 1.34e+08 threads (in launches of 16777216). Slowdown vs Option 3: 1.7x. Chunked launches of this many threads are cheap on this GPU, so the book's 'too many threads' concern is small here (it also assumed the G80).
  * Option 2 (M threads, atomics): 10.4x slower than Option 3. Its launch fills only 25 % of the warp capacity (launch fill above).
  * Staggering Option 2's start voxel changes its time by +14 %: spreading the atomics does not help, so same-voxel contention is not its main cost here (hypothesis, not tested: too few threads to hide latency, and atomic throughput).
  * Option 3 needs no atomics and keeps every voxel's sum in registers; it is the fastest mapping, as the book recommends.
